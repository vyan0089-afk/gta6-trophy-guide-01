Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough, and there is no mission replay — so keep a manual save before the finale. Work the stages in order; every stage names the trophies it unlocks.

  1. Stage 1 — Story, Chapters 1–4. Play the story straight through: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby!, Top Of The World, Ma, plus the named missions Trust, Break the Bank, Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked and Snow Storm. Meditate at every spot you find — the Chapter 1 spot is the one trophy other guides flag as possibly missable.
  2. Stage 2 — Final mission. Before the finale, save in a spare slot. Complete the final mission for the gold You Gotta Know When to Get Off. Free roam stays open afterwards.
  3. Stage 3 — Stranger strands & character finales. Finish 8 stranger strands for the silver Strangers & Freaks, including the finales for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret's hunt.
  4. Stage 4 — Activities. Try all 9 activity types (Master of None), gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist), win drone races at all 14 Top Fun locations, win 20 different races, beat Cal at fishing, finish an arcade game and bowl at Kolt & Associates.
  5. Stage 5 — Collectibles, with counts. 3 collectible card sets, WAiNK at 100 locations, 100 vehicles discovered, 18 meditation spots, 50 challenges.
  6. Stage 6 — Combat & heists. 250 stunts, 5 enemies in one Combat Focus, 5 bank vaults robbed and escaped, one armored truck, one illegal operation per gang (9 gangs), one jewelry store robbed undetected.
  7. Stage 7 — Exploration & economy. All 52 areas of Leonida, a cocktail on Ocean Beach, $250,000 laundered in marked bills, $100,000 spent in shops, all 8 garage properties, 10 vehicles registered to Scooter Bros.
  8. Stage 8 — Cleanup & choices. Sweep the missables list, finish the choice trophies, then chase A Long Road — 100% completion, the gold that takes the longest. The platinum Vice Got a Price! pops when the other 54 are done.

Counts in this roadmap come from the official trophy descriptions: 250 stunts · 9 activity types · 42 Shooting Range challenges · 14 drone race locations · 20 races · 8 stranger strands · 18 meditation spots · 3 card sets · 100 WAiNK locations · 50 challenges · 100 vehicles · 5 bank vaults · 52 areas · 9 gangs · 8 garages · 10 registrations · $250,000 laundered · $100,000 spent.

Missables — what to do before it is too late

There is no mission replay, so the rule is simple: do the flagged thing the first time the chance appears. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story. The one trophy other guides single out is Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot, starting in Chapter 1.
